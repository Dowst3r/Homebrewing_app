Settings

Recipes, databases and colours are saved on this device when you add or save them. They are ready when you reopen the app and after normal updates.

Personal colours

Save two personal colour palettes. Tap a colour box to choose a colour, or enter its red, green and blue numbers.

RGB means red, green and blue. Each number runs from 0 (none of that colour) to 255 (the most). Black is 0, 0, 0; white is 255, 255, 255; red is 255, 0, 0.

Preview your colours below. The app changes when you press Save and use palette.

Optional backup and restore

Download an extra copy for safekeeping or moving to another phone. You do not need to restore a backup when reopening or updating the app.
